# Marketing Positioning

## Category

Dataverse AI Action Platform is an autonomous enterprise intelligence layer for Microsoft Dataverse and Power Platform.

It is not just a Copilot extension or a Dataverse assistant. It is a governed execution and intelligence platform that turns enterprise intent into safe, explainable, auditable action.

## One-Line Positioning

Turn Dataverse from an app backend into a governed AI operating system for enterprise action, intelligence, and execution.

## Primary Audience

- Power Platform Center of Excellence
- Dataverse and Dynamics architects
- Enterprise IT governance teams
- Operations leaders
- Sales and service process owners
- PMO and transformation teams
- Citizen developer enablement teams

## Pain Points

- Business users are trapped in forms, views, menus, and manual workflows.
- Power Platform environments accumulate hidden dependencies, drift, and governance risk.
- Dataverse data is rich but hard to turn into operational intelligence.
- AI assistants often explain or draft, but do not provide governed execution.
- Bulk changes, approvals, and process automation are hard to trust without simulation and audit.

## Core Message

Dataverse AI Action Platform combines Dataverse semantics, OAuth-backed live metadata access, governance-first execution controls, AI action design, enterprise memory, predictive intelligence, and autonomous agent strategy in one Codex plugin.

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Intent Becomes Action

Users express business goals in natural language. The platform turns intent into Dataverse-aware plans, validations, confirmations, and execution paths.

### 2. Governance Is Built In

Every action is designed around permissions, approval thresholds, policy validation, simulation, audit, and explainability.

### 3. Dataverse Becomes Enterprise Intelligence

Tables and relationships become a cognitive graph of processes, roles, decisions, KPIs, risks, ownership, and history.

### 4. AI Moves From Assistant to Runtime

The long-term platform vision is enterprise intelligence as runtime: semantic understanding, autonomous process execution, predictive risk detection, and continuous organizational learning.

### 5. Trust Becomes a Product Feature

Every proposed action can be scored, simulated, explained, approved, and evidenced before it changes enterprise data.

## Elevator Pitch

Most Power Platform AI tools help users ask questions or draft content. Dataverse AI Action Platform is built for enterprise execution: it maps intent to Dataverse actions, simulates risk, enforces governance boundaries, explains decisions, and connects to live Dataverse metadata through OAuth-backed MCP tools.

## Sales Narrative

1. Start with the current pain: business workflows are fragmented across apps, tables, flows, approvals, and manual work.
2. Show the shift: users should express goals, not navigate systems.
3. Explain the trust model: every action is permission-aware, policy-aware, explainable, and auditable.
4. Prove the foundation: the plugin can inspect real Dataverse metadata through OAuth-backed MCP tools.
5. Expand the vision: cognitive graph, digital twin, predictive intelligence, enterprise memory, and autonomous agents.

## Proof Points To Build

- OAuth `WhoAmI` and metadata inspection against a sandbox Dataverse environment.
- Intent-to-action plan generated from a real business scenario.
- Simulation preview for a risky bulk update.
- ALM readiness review for a real solution.
- Executive intelligence brief generated from sample Dataverse data.
- Trust score generated for a proposed high-impact action.
- Change impact brief generated for a Dataverse table, flow, or security-role change.
- Tenant-specific skill blueprint generated from sample metadata and governance rules.

## Claims Guardrails

Use:

- "Designs governed AI actions."
- "Connects to Dataverse through OAuth-backed MCP tools."
- "Supports safety-gated writes."
- "Provides a roadmap toward autonomous enterprise intelligence."
- "Scores AI actions for trust, risk, evidence, and impact."
- "Documents compliance evidence requirements for AI-assisted actions."

Avoid until implemented and validated:

- "Fully autonomous production execution."
- "Automatically remediates governance violations."
- "Learns continuously from all enterprise systems."
- "Replaces Microsoft Copilot."
- "Guarantees compliance."
- "Automatically fixes production issues without approval."
- "Continuously learns from tenant data without governance controls."
