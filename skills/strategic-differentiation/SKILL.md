# Strategic Differentiation

## Purpose
Turn the PRD vision into clear enterprise positioning: not "AI for Power Apps", but a conversational enterprise execution platform.

Use this skill for:
- USP refinement
- investor or stakeholder messaging
- product differentiation against Microsoft Copilot
- app-centric versus intent-centric positioning
- roadmap framing
- executive summaries

## V1 Boundary
This skill creates product strategy and messaging guidance only. It does not make market claims that require external validation unless sources are provided or current research is performed.

## Workflow
1. Anchor the message in the core vision: authorized users execute enterprise actions in Dataverse through natural language.
2. Differentiate assistive copilot behavior from governed autonomous execution, architecture awareness, runtime awareness, and process orchestration.
3. Frame Dataverse as semantic enterprise graph, AI operating system, and enterprise memory layer where appropriate.
4. Convert features into business outcomes: reduced navigation, faster process completion, safer bulk operations, explainable decisions, and governed automation.
5. Keep claims enterprise-practical and avoid unsupported guarantees.

## Output Format
Return:
- `Core USP`: one sharp positioning sentence.
- `Differentiators`: clear comparison points.
- `Enterprise value`: operational, governance, and adoption benefits.
- `Proof points needed`: evidence required before external claims.
- `Recommended wording`: concise copy for plugin, PRD, pitch, or roadmap.
